Emergency Power Off Board

2026 / Hardware / KiCAD / SPICE / Cornell Hyperloop

Emergency power off for Cornell Hyperloop's pod. A Start pulse latches power on. Trigger a stop and the board cuts power to the full pod, and nothing comes back until Start is pressed again.

  • The stop switch opens
  • The controller asserts the stop line (Q1)
  • The enable signal or 12 V is lost, or the controller unplugs or resets
